About

A valuation partner built for lenders and institutions

Quantis Valuation is a woman-owned and minority-owned property valuation company based in New York. We manage the appraisal process from engagement to delivery, matching each assignment with a licensed appraiser who knows the local market.

We work with institutions of every size, including banks and credit unions that prefer not to order through traditional appraisal management channels, and we are building our practice to serve government and institutional clients.

Our focus is simple: credible, well-supported valuations, clear communication and dependable turnaround.

Services

Valuation services

Residential appraisals

Single-family, condominium and 2–4 unit properties for purchase, refinance, equity lending and other lending decisions.

Commercial appraisals

Valuation of income-producing and owner-occupied commercial property, completed by appropriately licensed appraisers.

Desktop & hybrid valuations

Alternative valuation products that combine data analysis with third-party property inspections where the lender's program allows.

Review appraisals

Desk and field reviews of existing appraisal reports to evaluate support, compliance and reasonableness of conclusions.

Portfolio valuations

Coordinated valuation of multiple properties for lenders, including loan portfolios, asset reviews and bulk assignments.

Government & institutional

Valuation support for public agencies and institutions, prepared to the documentation and reporting requirements of each engagement.
